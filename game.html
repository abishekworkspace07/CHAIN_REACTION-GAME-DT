GAME RULES

  • CHAIN RXN. is a turn-based strategy game played on a grid, where players compete to dominate the board using explosive chain reactions.

  • Game Setup : The number of players and grid/board size has already been chosen. Each cell on the board has a capacity determined by the number of orthogonally adjacent neighbours it has. Corner cells have a capacity of 2, edge cells have a capacity of 3, and inner cells have a capacity of 4. A cell can safely hold up to one less than its capacity; once the capacity is reached, the cell explodes.

  • Placement : At the start of the game, players may place elements only on empty cells. The players will place "one-less than capacity" number of elements in a cell and gain control. After gaining control of a cell, a player may place additional elements only on cells they already own. Every placement increases the number of elements in that cell by one.

  • Explode on Cap : When a cell reaches its capacity, an explosion is triggered. The exploding cell becomes empty and distributes one element to each orthogonally adjacent cell (up, down, left, and right). These neighbouring cells may themselves reach capacity and explode, creating powerful chain reactions across the board. Any cell affected by an explosion immediately changes ownership to the player who caused the explosion, allowing players to capture enemy territory and rapidly shift control of the board through strategic reactions.

  • The game is played in turns, with each player required to make their move within a limited turn timer. In addition, the entire match is governed by a global game timer to maintain fast-paced gameplay and constant pressure.

  • Points : Players earn points based on the number of elements with them and the number of chain reactions triggered during the game. If a player's timer ends or they have no more elements, they are eliminated from the game. In case of player timeout, the player's elements are preserved for other players to capture

  • Elimination : If a player's timer ends or they have no more elements, they are eliminated from the game.

  • End : The match ends when only one player's elements remain on the board, or when the game timer ends. In the event of a game timeout, the player with the higher points is declared the winner.
